Bathtime Shooting Range

THE SHORT VERSION
Name the targets. Set a timer. Squirt everything in sight.
Bath time is already wet. Might as well make it a game.

The tub has targets everywhere and your kid already has a squirt gun. The faucet, the knobs, the shampoo bottle, the rubber duck, the soap dish. Name them, point the timer at the wall, and let them go. They will not want to get out.

What you need
⏱Setup: 0 min
🧹Mess: Bath mess only
⚡Energy: Active

A squirt gun, squirt toy, or any bath toy that sprays. A timer. A bathtub full of targets.

Steps
1

Before the bath or right at the start, name the targets out loud together. The faucet is one. The hot knob. The cold knob. The shampoo bottle. The soap dish. The rubber duck. The back wall. Give each one a point value if they are old enough to care.

2

Set a timer. 60 seconds works well. Younger kids can go longer. Call the first target and let them start squirting. Switch targets every 10 to 15 seconds so it stays moving.

3

You call the targets. They squirt. When the timer runs out, tally the hits. If they are playing solo, they are beating their own score. If there are two kids, they take turns on the same targets and compare.

4

Run a second round. Let them pick the target order this time. The kid who controls the target list immediately becomes a different kind of focused.

Variations
Speed round

30 seconds, five targets in order as fast as possible. No mistakes, no skipping. Do it again and try to beat the time.

Boss target

One target is worth five points. Everything else is worth one. They pick the boss target before the timer starts. Changes the whole strategy.

Two-kid tournament

Same target, both squirting at once. First one to hit it three times wins the round. Best of five rounds wins the championship. Extremely chaotic.

Tiny kid version

No timer, no points. Just say a target name and let them squirt it. Celebrate every hit. That is the whole game at age 2.

Parent tip: The water that ends up outside the tub is going to happen. Put a towel down before you start and stop thinking about it. The kid who does not want to get out of the bath is worth a wet bathroom floor.
